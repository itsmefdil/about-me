# OpenInfra Indonesia

**OpenInfra Indonesia** is the official Indonesian user group of the OpenInfra Foundation, dedicated to open source infrastructure technologies such as OpenStack, Kata Containers, StarlingX, and Zuul.

As a **Volunteer Organizer**, Fadilah Riczky actively contributes to planning community meetups, coordinating speaker invitations, and supporting local chapter events across Indonesia.
